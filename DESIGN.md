# Fathom Field Systems

Fathom turns spatial, radio and acoustic measurements into numbered observations.
The identity is a field instrument, with no affiliation to an agency, military or game.

## Visual system

Keep the original three-contour F mark, custom FATHOM lettering and 24 px icon family.
Graphite (#101114), optical white (#f4f4f5), signal orange (#f48142), neutral steel
(#a4a5a8). No green cast. Orange means selection or an observed event; colour never
claims an unknown object is dangerous. Sofia Sans is the primary face, JetBrains Mono
is for measurements, timestamps and hashes. Range imagery uses neutral white-to-steel
values. Ground-truth class colours belong only to labelled simulations.

## Working screen

The live measurement field leads. Compact Chase/Plan/Side controls sit inside it;
Expand view removes the control rail and observation register while keeping transport
and the range image. Hardware is the default source. The microphone is offered as a
real immediately available input; simulation is a secondary, labelled action.
The numbered observation links a track, log row and timeline entry.

The CPU renderer draws a bounded sample of received points with the same orbit, zoom,
colour and selection controls. Its label discloses sampled rendering. It invents no
scene. Full acquisition, range image and recording are independent of the renderer.
Connection, calibration and stale-input states must remain distinct.

## Motion and audio

Use 160 ms state transitions, stable measurements and reduced-motion alternatives.
The optional 6.4-second intro resolves concentric apertures into the original mark.
Escape and Skip stop both animation and scheduled sound. Field cues are short;
Cinematic adds licensed samples and layered transients. Warning audio is finite,
rate-limited and tied to the user-configured proximity threshold. It is not a threat
classification. Audio is enabled by a gesture and individual events can be muted.
